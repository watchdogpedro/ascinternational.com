## How to read this

- **Title** column shows the full title as Google sees it. The site auto-adds `| ASC International` to every page, so the editable part is what comes before that. Target: 60 characters or under.
- **Description** target: 160 characters or under.
- `(n)` after each line is its character count. Current values that are over the limit are the reason the page is listed.
- I cut the marketing throat-clearing ("Comprehensive guide to", "Discover why", "Learn how to", "Advanced", "Revolutionary") and kept the concrete parts — model names, numbers, standards. Length counts assume plain hyphens; a few specs use inch/µm marks that render fine on the live pages.
